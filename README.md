### 🛡️ Aegis — Parametric Wage Insurance for Gig Workers

[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Aegis-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/harish040120/Aegis)
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Guidewire_DEVTrails_2026_Phase_3-gold?style=flat-square)
![Stack](https://img.shields.io/badge/Stack-Flutter_·_FastAPI_·_React_·_PostgreSQL_·_XGBoost_·_Razorpay-blueviolet?style=flat-square)

> *"Every 60 seconds, a scheduler wakes up and asks: does Shiva need help right now? If yes — the money moves before he even knows to ask."*

India's 5M+ gig delivery workers lose 20–30% of monthly income to rain, AQI spikes, and civic disruptions with zero safety net. **Aegis** solves this with **parametric insurance** — no claims, no paperwork, just automatic UPI payouts the moment a verifiable disruption trigger fires.

- 🤖 **60-second auto-trigger loop** (APScheduler + XGBoost + Isolation Forest) detects disruption events, validates income impact, and fires Razorpay payouts — fully autonomously, across multi-platform workers (Zomato + Swiggy)
- 🔐 **3-layer fraud engine** (Behavioral ML + Rule Engine + GNN ring detection) flags GPS spoofing and coordinated fraud rings before a single rupee moves

📄 [View Pitch Deck](https://github.com/harish040120/Aegis/blob/main/TEAM%20ZERO%20NOISE%20CREW.pdf) · `FastAPI` `Flutter` `React 19` `PostgreSQL` `scikit-learn` `Razorpay` `OpenWeatherMap` `WAQI`
